# What to post next: a content brief for a home cake decorator (written 23 September 2026)

## Summary

**Post first: a black-and-silver "gothic lace" vintage heart cake.** Post it as a short reel, plus a carousel on how to make black buttercream, plus 4–5 fresh Pinterest pins. Get it up this week or next.

Why this cake, why now:
- **Halloween is five weeks away.** Instagram content can still land in time. On Pinterest you're already late, so post those pins immediately.
- **Heart and vintage cakes are still growing, not fading.** One London bakery's Jan–Aug 2026 sales show heart cakes up from 4.4% to 6.0% of orders and vintage/Lambeth up from 3.6% to 5.0%. Lambeth is the frilly, layered piping style you already do.
- **The trend is heading darker.** Pinterest's 2026 forecast includes "Vamp Romantic" (gothic, after-dark) and "Laced Up" (lace searches up 105–215%). Decorators are also predicting silver replacing gold this year.
- **The earlier Halloween Lambeth tutorials are dated.** The best-known one (Sugar & Sparrow, 2023) is orange and purple with sprinkles. A moody black cake with lace piping looks like this year.

After that, the next 8 weeks rotate three kinds of post:
1. Seasonal hero cakes: Halloween, then Christmas, then Valentine's hearts. Christmas and Valentine's go on Pinterest well before the date.
2. Two trend cakes almost nobody is making yet: an **oval "cameo" cake** and a **prize-rosette ribbon cake**.
3. An evergreen series that fixes the gap between "homemade" and "bakery-made" (your whole promise). These are the saves and shares that keep working after the season ends.

Your core style isn't on its way out, so you don't need to change direction. What you need is **the next version of it**, not more of the same pastel heart.

### Where vintage is heading in 2026
These come up again and again across decorator forecasts:
- **Darker, bolder colours used tone-on-tone** (piping the same colour as the base): chartreuse, deep red, plum, and **silver accents after years of gold** ([<creator>](<creator’s page>)).
- **Black, deep red and moody piping.** The gothic Lambeth look is established ([The Nod Mag](https://thenodmag.com/content/vintage-rococo-cakes-baking-food-trends), [Butter Studio](https://thebutterstudio.com/the-2025-christmas-cake-trends-you-need-to-know/)).
- **Lace, doily and crochet-like piping** ([The Knot / AOL](https://www.theknot.com/content/wedding-cake-trends), [The Cake Decorating Co.](https://www.thecakedecoratingcompany.co.uk/blogs/cake-decorating-ideas/introducing-the-cake-trends-were-obsessed-with)).
- **Oval "cameo/locket" cakes** and short, wide cakes ([THE WED](https://thewed.com/magazine/Pinterest-predicts-wed-cake-food-trends-2026), [<creator>](<creator’s page>)).
- **Baroque and classical motifs** such as shells, swans and columns; **polka dots on vintage hearts**; **bows everywhere**; **all-white "Cloud Dancer" cakes** ([Cake Decorating Co.](https://www.thecakedecoratingcompany.co.uk/blogs/cake-decorating-ideas/introducing-the-cake-trends-were-obsessed-with), [Gloria's](https://www.gloriascakeandcandy.com/blogs/news/viral-cake-trends-cake-decorating)).
- **Sculpted cakes that copy real objects**, like a jewellery box or shoes ([THE WED](https://thewed.com/magazine/major-cake-trends-for-2026)). This suits your "odd sculpted cake".
- **Flavours:** matcha more than doubled (2.7% → 6.3% of orders). Pistachio is steady at about 15% rather than new. Earl Grey and brown butter are being tipped.

### Pinterest search signals that match your style
From [Pinterest Predicts 2026](https://newsroom.pinterest.com/news/pinterest-predicts-nonconformity-self-preservation-and-escapism-drive-21-trends-for-2026/) and the [fall 2026 Hobbies report](https://newsroom.pinterest.com/news/2026-pinterest-hobbies-trend-report/):
- **Laced Up:** lace nails +215%, lace doily +105% ([FoodShot summary](https://foodshot.ai/blog/pinterest-food-trends)). This translates directly to lace piping.
- **Vamp Romantic:** dark romantic makeup +160%, gothic nails +180%. This is your black-and-red Halloween cake.
- **Brooched:** brooch aesthetic +110%. A piped brooch works as the centrepiece of a cameo cake.
- **Cool Blue** (ice blue +50–55%) and **Extra Celestial** (opalescent +115%). This points to a silver-and-ice-blue winter cake.
- **Weekend Equestrian:** "rosette ribbon" +3,816%, the largest jump in the report. The flat round shape of a prize rosette is basically ruffle piping. I couldn't find anyone making a rosette-ribbon cake, so this is a real gap.
- **Kitchen Witch** / *Practical Magic 2* tie-in. The film [opened 10 September to a weak $30M](https://variety.com/2026/film/box-office/practical-magic-2-box-office-opening-weekend-disappointment-1236860432/), so don't build a post around the film. The witchy-kitchen look is still usable for Halloween styling.

### What to skip
- **Burn-away cakes:** sales collapsed.
- **Cartoon Halloween cakes** (Frankenstein, pumpkin faces): crowded and not your brand.
- **Mystic Outlands** styling: even food-photography advice says misty, moody forest scenes "kill appetite cues".

## 2. Platform facts that shape the formats

**Instagram**
- Adam Mosseri, who runs Instagram, has named watch time, **sends per reach** (how often people DM your post to someone) and likes per reach as the top signals. Replies and DM conversations were added as a ranked signal in March 2026 ([Dataslayer](https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers), [Sprout](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/instagram-algorithm/)). Design posts people want to *send to a baker friend*.
- Carousels now allow **up to 20 slides**. Put your strongest image first, because Instagram may show a carousel again, starting from slide 2, to people who didn't swipe.
- Reels reach the most non-followers. Third-party guides suggest **under 30 seconds** for discovery.
- **Trial Reels** (shown only to non-followers first) can now be scheduled. Use them to test two hooks on the same footage.
- The **originality rules** tightened on 30 April 2026. Accounts that mostly repost lose recommendations ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/04/30/instagram-removes-algorithm-recommendations-repost-content-aggregator/)). This works in your favour since everything you post is your own. Just don't repost other people's cakes as "inspo" posts.
- Caveat: the specific multipliers you'll see in these blogs (e.g. "sends = 3–5× likes") are third-party estimates. Treat them as rough direction, not official numbers.

**Pinterest**
- Pinterest now ranks on **how well a pin matches a search, plus saves and freshness**. Keywords in pin titles, descriptions and board names matter far more than hashtags. Use **2:3 vertical** images (1000×1500) ([Outfy](https://www.outfy.com/blog/pinterest-algorithm/), [Madpin](https://madpinmedia.com/what-changed-in-pinterest-algorithm/)).
- Pinterest recognises near-duplicate images. So **make several different pins per cake** (finished cake, close-up, tip diagram, before/after) rather than re-pinning one image.
- **Seasonal pins need 30–90 days to rank**, which sets your Pinterest calendar:
  - Halloween: post now (late).
  - Christmas: mid-to-late October.
  - **Valentine's: late November to December.** This is your biggest heart-cake moment of the year. Don't wait until February.

## 4. Post specs

### Post 1: Gothic lace vintage heart (Halloween hero)
- **The cake:** black buttercream heart, piped tone-on-tone black plus **deep red**, with a **silver lace** layer piped over the top. Cornelli lace is a freeform squiggle pattern piped with a small round tip; a doily-style scallop border also works. Finish with fresh red cherries or silver pearl sprinkles (dragées).
  - Skip the skull sprinkles. You want it to read as grown-up and romantic, not a kids' Halloween cake.
  - Silver instead of gold separates it from the gold-on-black versions already circulating and matches the 2026 silver trend.
- **Reel, 20–30 seconds.**
  - Open on the finished cake being turned in moody light.
  - Hard cut to the black buttercream coming out of the bowl, then the piping sequence, speeded up.
  - End on the lace close-up.
  - On-screen hook, tested as two Trial Reels: *"The vintage heart, but make it gothic"* vs *"Black buttercream that doesn't taste bitter or stain your teeth →"*.
- **Companion carousel, 8–10 slides: "True black buttercream without the bitterness."** This is a real pain point; the web is full of pages on it.
  - Start from chocolate or black cocoa buttercream so you need far less gel colour.
  - Use a concentrated gel colour.
  - Let it darken overnight, or warm a small portion in the microwave to speed it up.
  - Say which brand you use.
  - Last slide: *"Send this to the friend who's making a Halloween cake."*
  - References: [Sugar & Sparrow](https://sugarandsparrow.com/black-buttercream-recipe/), [Chelsweets](https://chelsweets.com/how-to-make-black-frosting/), [Sugarologie](https://www.sugarologie.com/recipes/black-frosting).
- **Pinterest titles:** "Gothic Black Vintage Heart Cake – Lambeth Piping Halloween Cake Idea"; "How to Make Black Buttercream (Not Bitter, Won't Stain)"; "Black and Red Lace Halloween Cake for Adults".

### Post 2: The oval cameo cake (trend plus a light carve)
- **Why:** oval is the shape named most often across 2026 forecasts ("cameo pendant / vintage locket"), and **no one is teaching how to make one at home without a special tin**. Blogs have already done that job for heart cakes ([example](https://www.itsalwaysautumn.com/four-easy-ways-make-heart-shaped-cake-without-special-pan.html)); ovals are open.
- **The cake:** all-white or cream (the "Cloud Dancer" white trend). Pipe a scalloped cameo frame in the centre and a **piped brooch** inside it (the Brooched trend), or lily of the valley. Pearl borders.
- **Carousel: "No oval tin? Cut one from a 9×13."**
  - Include a printable template drawn to scale.
  - Show the cuts, stacking, and crumb coat.
  - Pinterest will likely save this one over and over.
- **Reel:** a satisfying shot of the template-guided cut, then the cameo frame being piped.

### Post 3: Prize-rosette ribbon cake (the one nobody's doing yet)
- **Why:** "rosette ribbon" is up **+3,816%** on Pinterest. It's the biggest single increase in its fall 2026 report. I found no one making it as a cake, and it plays straight to your piping skills.
- **The cake:**
  - A round cake piped as a prize rosette: concentric rows of ruffles or pleats (104 or French tips) out to the edge.
  - A centre disc with a piped "1st", "Best Mum" or age.
  - Two ribbon tails on the board. Make them from fondant, wafer paper, or real ribbon under acetate.
- **Angle:** "the birthday cake for whoever deserves a prize." That makes it sendable, and it's a personalised message cake, which is one of the 2026 birthday trends.
- **Risk:** the search spike is an equestrian fashion trend, so the crossover to cakes isn't proven. Test it on a Trial Reel first. If it lands, you'll be the first to own it.

### Post 4: "6 things that make a cake look homemade (and the fix for each)"
This is **your brand promise in one post** and should be your most-sent format. Suggested "tells":
1. Uneven swag and shell spacing. Fix: a parchment circle folded into 8 as a template ([method](https://sugarandsparrow.com/halloween-lambeth-cake-tutorial/)).
2. Crumbs showing through. Fix: crumb coat, then chill.
3. Loud, straight-from-the-bottle colours. Fix: mute them with a touch of brown or the opposite colour.
4. A bare, messy base with the board showing. Fix: finish the bottom with a border.
5. Leaning or domed layers.
6. Piping that sags. Fix: consistency and temperature (see Post 6).

Show before/after pairs, one tell per slide. Turn it into a series: *"Homemade → bakery-made, part 1/2/3."*

### Post 5: Christmas vintage cake (live on Pinterest by early November)
- Deep red and forest green Lambeth piping with bows. This combination is well documented for the holidays ([Butter Studio](https://thebutterstudio.com/the-2025-christmas-cake-trends-you-need-to-know/)).
- Or a more distinctive option: **ice blue, white and silver with opalescent lustre dust** (Cool Blue plus Extra Celestial). It covers "winter birthday" searches through January, not just Christmas.
- Pin it by 1–7 November. Some Christmas pins should go up in mid-October using older work.

### Post 6: "Why your ruffles go limp"
- A diagnostic carousel on the problems people actually hit: buttercream too soft, a warm kitchen, hot hands, the bag overfilled.
- Fixes: chill for 10–15 minutes between layers, stir over an ice bath, fill the bag no more than two-thirds, and show what the right consistency looks like on a spatula ([CakeWhiz](https://cakewhiz.com/ruffle-cake/), [Cake Geek](https://cake-geek.com/index.php/how-to-pipe-buttercream-ruffles-frills/)).
- Pair it with a **"5 tips that do 90% of vintage piping"** reference slide: 1M, 4B or 32, 104, 16, and a #2 or #3 round. Save-bait and a reusable Pinterest pin ([tip guide](https://iscreamforbuttercream.com/favorite-buttercream-piping-tips-how-to-use-them/)).

## 5. How to film each cake so it becomes several posts
Every hero cake should produce:
- 1 reel under 30 seconds, with a second hook tested as a Trial Reel.
- 1 teaching carousel of 8–12 slides, with the best image first and a "send to…" line at the end.
- 1 Story with a poll or question box. Conversations and replies are now a ranked signal, and the answers tell you what tutorial to make next.
- 4–6 **different** 2:3 pins: hero, macro close-up, tip diagram, before/after, template. Each gets a keyword title.

**What to track:** sends and saves per reach on Instagram, and outbound saves and clicks on Pinterest. After 8 weeks, drop whichever of the three kinds of post (seasonal, trend, pain-point fix) earns the fewest sends.

**Caveat:** I can't see your analytics. If one of your past posts clearly beat the rest, look at its topic and format before following this calendar strictly.
