The recipe is hook, orange thread, optional fine gold wire, mottled brown partridge [S29]. Use your own recipe. The only text is the pattern name.

**Slides:** each shows the same hook from the same fixed camera position, in the construction from §2, entry 1. Only what was added this step gets one accent-colour outline, the same colour every slide. A small box in the corner lists only what this step uses.
- **2:** thread body wound. Box: thread.
- **3:** prepare the feather and tie it in. Dark Skies shows this as its own three-image sequence: stroke the fibres back, cut the tie-in point, wrap [S9]. Box: feather.

**Caption job:** ask what the first fly they ever tied was. This is my inference: it tells you who is a beginner, and it gives you the next post.

Draw arrows on the photo only at the differences that are visible in the photo: wing, hackle, weight, whichever your two actually differ by.
